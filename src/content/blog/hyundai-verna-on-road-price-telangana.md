## What Makes Up the Hyundai Verna On-Road Price in Telangana?

When we talk about the Hyundai Verna on-road price in Telangana, it's a combination of several factors. The ex-showroom price is the base cost, set by Hyundai. Then, you add the mandatory charges specific to our state. Here’s a breakdown:

1.  **Ex-Showroom Price:** This is the manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP) for the car, excluding taxes and registration. For the Hyundai Verna, the ex-showroom price starts from Rs 10.90 Lakhs.
2.  **RTO (Road Tax & Registration Charges):** This is a state government levy for registering your vehicle. In Telangana, the road tax usually ranges from 10% to 13% of the ex-showroom price, depending on the car's value. Higher-priced variants attract slightly higher percentages.
3.  **Insurance Cost:** Mandatory for all new vehicles. This covers potential damages or liabilities. The cost depends on the variant, chosen coverage (comprehensive, third-party), and the insurer. We always recommend comprehensive insurance for peace of mind.
4.  **FasTag & Logistics/Handling Charges:** Minor charges for the electronic toll collection tag and dealer handling.

Understanding these components helps you budget accurately. At Prakash Hyundai, we provide a detailed, itemized quote so there are no surprises.
