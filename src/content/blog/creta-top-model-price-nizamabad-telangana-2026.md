### Breaking Down the On-Road Price for Creta SX(O)

The on-road price isn't just the sticker price; it includes several mandatory components specific to our state, Telangana. Here’s a general breakdown for the Creta SX(O) as of August 2026:

*   **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base price set by Hyundai. For the Creta, it starts from Rs 11.11 Lakhs, but the SX(O) will obviously be significantly higher, typically in the Rs 15.50 - 18.50 Lakhs range depending on engine/transmission.
*   **Road Tax (RTO Registration):** In Telangana, this is a significant component, roughly 10-13% of the ex-showroom price, varying slightly based on the vehicle's value.
*   **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is mandatory. This cost varies based on the insurer, your chosen add-ons (like zero depreciation), and your no-claim bonus status. Expect this to be a substantial figure for a top-end SUV.
*   **TCS (Tax Collected at Source):** Applicable on vehicles above a certain price threshold.
*   **FasTag:** Mandatory for toll payments.
*   **Accessories/Extended Warranty (Optional):** Many customers opt for these, adding to the final cost but enhancing ownership experience.

Considering all these factors, the Creta SX(O) on-road price in Nizamabad for 2026 is estimated to be between **Rs 18.50 Lakhs and Rs 22.00 Lakhs**. Please contact us directly at Prakash Hyundai for today's exact details, as prices can fluctuate with policy changes and manufacturer updates.
