## The 147-Point Quality Check: A Deep Dive

The cornerstone of the Hyundai Promise certification is a comprehensive 147-point inspection. This isn't a quick once-over; it's a detailed examination conducted by trained Hyundai technicians. They scrutinize every major component and system of the car. Here’s what it typically covers:

### Engine & Transmission Inspection

Performance, leaks, fluid levels, clutch operation, gear shifts are all thoroughly checked.

### Brake System Evaluation

Pads, discs, calipers, fluid, and handbrake effectiveness are meticulously inspected.

### Suspension & Steering Assessment

Shock absorbers, linkages, power steering function, and alignment are carefully examined.

### Electrical Systems Test

Lights, indicators, horn, power windows, central locking, AC function, and infotainment system are all verified for proper operation.

### Exterior Condition Report

Body panels are checked for dents, scratches, rust; paint quality, tire condition (tread depth, wear), and alloy wheels are all part of the assessment.

### Interior Quality Check

Seat condition, upholstery, dashboard, controls, seat belts, carpet, and boot space are all scrutinized for wear and tear.

### Underbody Examination

The exhaust system, chassis, fuel tank, and any signs of major damage or repair are thoroughly inspected.

### Documentation Verification

Verification of service history, odometer reading, and legal papers ensures a transparent past.
