## What Exactly Influences the Creta On-Road Price Top Model?

At our Nizamabad showroom, the question we hear most often from customers looking at the Creta is, "What's the final price I'll pay?" It's never just the ex-showroom price you see in ads. The on-road price is a combination of several factors, and understanding each component is key to making an informed decision. For a premium SUV like this variant, these costs add up significantly.

Let's break down what goes into the final on-road price for this Hyundai SUV's top variant in Nizamabad:

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base price set by Hyundai India. For the Creta, starting prices are around Rs 11.11 Lakh, but the top model will be considerably higher.
2.  **Road Tax (RTO Registration):** This is a mandatory tax levied by the State Government. In Telangana, RTO charges are typically in the range of 10-13% of the ex-showroom price, varying slightly based on the car's price slab. This is a substantial part of the on-road cost.
3.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive motor insurance is compulsory. The cost depends on the Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the car, the chosen policy (zero depreciation, add-ons), and the insurance provider. For a top-end Creta, this will be a significant amount.
4.  **TCS (Tax Collected at Source):** A 1% TCS is applicable on vehicle purchases exceeding Rs 10 Lakh.
5.  **Fastag:** Mandatory for toll payments, this small charge is usually included.
6.  **Accessories & Extended Warranty:** While optional, many customers opt for genuine Hyundai accessories or an extended warranty for peace of mind. These add to the final bill.
7.  **Logistics/Handling Charges:** Some dealers might include minor charges for vehicle handling and transportation. We ensure transparency in all such costs.
