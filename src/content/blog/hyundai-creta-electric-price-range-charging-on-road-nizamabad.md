## Hyundai Creta Electric On-Road Price in Telangana

The ex-showroom price is just one part of the equation. For our customers in Nizamabad, Adilabad, or Bhainsa, understanding the on-road price is crucial. The on-road price includes:

1.  **Ex-Showroom Price:** The manufacturer's price without taxes or RTO charges.
2.  **Road Tax (RTO):** In Telangana, this typically ranges from 10-13% of the ex-showroom price, varying based on the vehicle's cost.
3.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive motor insurance is mandatory and varies based on the vehicle's value, chosen insurer, and add-ons.
4.  **Registration Charges:** Fees for vehicle registration.
5.  **Fastag, HSRP:** Minor charges for these mandatory accessories.

Generally, you can estimate the on-road price in Telangana by adding approximately 10-13% to the ex-showroom price. So, if the ex-showroom **Creta Electric price** is ₹20 Lakhs, the on-road price could be in the range of ₹22 Lakhs to ₹22.6 Lakhs.
