## Understanding the Creta SX On Road Price in Telangana (As of July 2026)

When you are shopping for a mid-size SUV in Nizamabad, looking only at the ex-showroom price can give you an incomplete picture. The actual **creta sx on road price** is what you pay to drive the car out of our showroom and onto the roads of Armoor, Bodhan, or Kamareddy.

In Telangana, the road tax structure is calculated based on the ex-showroom price of the vehicle, engine capacity, and fuel type. For a premium SUV like the Creta, which starts at an ex-showroom price of Rs 11.11L for the entry-level E variant, the tax rate typically ranges from 10% to 13%. This variance depends on whether it is registered under an individual's name or a company's name, and whether you already own another passenger vehicle.

To give you a clearer estimate, let us look at the components that make up the final on-road price as of July 2026:

1. **Ex-Showroom Price:** The base price set by Hyundai India.
2. **Telangana State Road Tax & Registration:** This is a direct payment to the Regional Transport Authority (RTA) in Nizamabad or your local district RTA.
3. **Insurance:** Includes 1-year Own Damage (OD) and 3-year Third-Party (TP) insurance, which is mandatory. We highly recommend adding zero-depreciation, engine protection, and return-to-invoice covers to safeguard your investment.
4. **TCS (Tax Collected at Source):** A mandatory 1% tax collected on all vehicles priced above Rs 10 Lakhs.
5. **Fastag & Ancillaries:** Standard charges for toll transit and basic registration handling.

Because these components can change slightly based on the latest municipal guidelines, we always advise buyers to call for today's exact price before finalizing their budget.
