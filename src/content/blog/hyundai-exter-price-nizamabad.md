## Understanding the Hyundai Exter Price in Nizamabad (As of July 2026)

When buying a new car in Northern Telangana, understanding how the "ex-showroom" price translates to the final "on-road" price is crucial. The ex-showroom price of the base Hyundai Exter starts at Rs. 6.13 Lakh. However, the final price you pay to drive the vehicle home from our Prakash Hyundai showroom includes several mandatory and optional charges.

In Telangana, the on-road price is calculated by adding approximately 10% to 13% to the ex-showroom price. This percentage covers:

1. **Telangana State Road Tax (Life Tax):** Calculated based on the vehicle's engine capacity and ex-showroom cost. For individual registration of cars under Rs. 10 Lakh, the life tax is generally lower than for vehicles priced above that threshold.
2. **RTO Registration and Green Tax:** Official charges levied by the Nizamabad RTO (TS-16) or neighboring offices like Kamareddy (TS-17) and Adilabad (TS-01).
3. **Comprehensive Insurance:** Includes 1-year Own Damage and 3-year Third-Party cover, which is highly recommended for protecting your new assets on busy highways like NH 44.
4. **Fastag and Handling Charges:** Standardized government-mandated toll tags.

If you are comparing the Exter with other models in our lineup, you might also want to look at the larger [Hyundai Venue](/cars/venue) which starts at Rs. 7.94 Lakh ex-showroom, or the spacious [Hyundai Creta](/cars/creta) starting at Rs. 11.11 Lakh ex-showroom, depending on your budget and space needs.
