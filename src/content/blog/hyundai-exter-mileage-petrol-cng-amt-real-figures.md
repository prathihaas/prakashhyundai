## Understanding Hyundai Exter Fuel Economy: Petrol vs. CNG

The Exter comes with a reliable 1.2-litre Kappa petrol engine, available with either a 5-speed manual transmission (MT) or a 5-speed Automated Manual Transmission (AMT). For those looking for even lower running costs, there's also a factory-fitted CNG option, exclusively with the manual gearbox. Each variant has its own efficiency characteristics, and understanding these is key to choosing the right vehicle for your needs.

### Official ARAI vs. Real-World Fuel Economy

Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) figures are standardized test results, offering a benchmark. However, real-world conditions – traffic, road quality, driving style, and even the use of AC – significantly impact how much fuel your car consumes. In Nizamabad, with its mix of city traffic and occasional highway stretches, real-world numbers are what truly matter.

## Hyundai Exter Petrol Mileage (MT & AMT)

The petrol variants of the Exter are quite efficient. The 1.2L engine is known for its smooth performance and decent power delivery, making it suitable for both city driving and highway cruises.

### Manual Transmission (MT) Figures

For the manual petrol Exter, ARAI certifies it at 19.4 kmpl. From our customer feedback and observations on local roads, drivers typically report figures ranging from **17 kmpl to 20 kmpl**. This variation depends heavily on factors like how heavy-footed one is, how much time is spent in bumper-to-bumper Nizamabad traffic, and whether they're driving primarily in town or on the open highway towards Adilabad.

### Automated Manual Transmission (AMT) Figures

The AMT version offers the convenience of an automatic without a significant drop in fuel efficiency. ARAI rates the petrol AMT at 19.2 kmpl. In practice, our customers find it delivers between **16.5 kmpl and 19.5 kmpl**. The AMT's intelligent gear shifts often help maintain good efficiency, especially for those who prefer an easier drive in city conditions.

## Hyundai Exter CNG Mileage

The Exter's CNG variant is a smart choice for those prioritising ultra-low running costs. Hyundai's factory-fitted CNG kit is seamlessly integrated, ensuring safety and optimal performance.

### CNG Manual Transmission Figures

ARAI certifies the Exter CNG at an impressive 27.1 km/kg. In real-world driving conditions around Nizamabad, including trips to nearby towns like Bodhan or Banswada, owners frequently report figures between **24 km/kg and 28 km/kg**. This makes the CNG Exter one of the most economical options in its segment, significantly reducing your fuel bills.

### Why CNG is a Smart Choice in Telangana

With the rising fuel prices, CNG offers a substantially cheaper alternative. The cost per kilometre is significantly lower, and with a growing network of CNG stations, it’s becoming increasingly practical for daily use and even longer journeys within the state.

## Factors Affecting Your Exter's Fuel Economy

Several elements influence how much fuel your vehicle consumes. Being aware of these can help you maximise its efficiency.

### Driving Style

Aggressive acceleration and sudden braking consume more fuel. A smooth, consistent driving style, anticipating traffic, and maintaining steady speeds will always yield better results. This is especially true on the busy roads of Nizamabad.

### Road Conditions and Traffic

Hilly terrains, poorly maintained roads, and heavy traffic (common during peak hours near the bus stand) force the engine to work harder, decreasing efficiency. The Exter's 185mm ground clearance helps navigate rough patches, but continuous stop-and-go driving will always impact economy.

### Maintenance

Regular servicing, as recommended by Hyundai, is crucial. Things like properly inflated tyres, clean air filters, and correct engine oil levels directly impact fuel consumption.
