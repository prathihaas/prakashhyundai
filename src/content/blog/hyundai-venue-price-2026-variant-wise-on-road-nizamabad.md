Because the Venue sits comfortably below the Rs 10 Lakh ex-showroom mark for most variants, your tax burden is relatively low compared to larger SUVs.

## Venue Variant Guide: Which One Should You Buy?

Choosing the right variant is just as important as knowing the hyundai venue price. Here is our dealer expertise to help you decide:

### Venue E 1.2 Petrol — The Smart Entry
If you are upgrading from a [Grand i10 Nios](/cars/grand-i10-nios) and want an SUV body on a strict budget, the E variant is your starting point. It gives you dual airbags, ABS with EBD, and an 8-inch touchscreen with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. It misses out on rear wipers and premium seat fabrics, but mechanically, it is identical to the higher-spec 1.2L variants.

### Venue S 1.2 Petrol — The Value Sweet Spot
Priced at Rs 9.20 Lakh ex-showroom, this is where the Venue starts making sense. You get voice-activated smart features, rear wiper and defogger, and day/night IRVM. For a family in Nizamabad looking for a safe, city-friendly compact SUV without paying for turbo power, the S variant is our top recommendation.

### Venue SX(O) Turbo — The Enthusiast's Pick
If your budget crosses Rs 12 Lakh ex-showroom, skip the naturally aspirated engines and jump straight to the SX(O) Turbo. You get 6 airbags, a Bose premium sound system, a panoramic sunroof, and the 7-speed DCT automatic (if you stretch to the top variant). It competes directly with the Kia Sonet and Maruti Grand Vitara but offers a more premium interior feel.
