## Variant-Wise Breakdown: Does Wheel Size Affect the Exter Ground Clearance 2026?

Many buyers ask us at Prakash Hyundai if opting for the base variant with smaller wheels reduces the ground clearance. The short answer is no. 

1. **Lower Variants (EX, EX+, S):** These run on 14-inch steel wheels with 165/70 R14 tyres. 
2. **Higher Variants (SX, SX(O), SX(O) Connect):** These feature 15-inch alloy wheels with 175/65 R15 tyres. 

Because the tyre sidewall profile on the 14-inch wheel is taller, the overall outer diameter (rolling circumference) of both wheel setups is almost identical. Therefore, the **exter ground clearance 2026** remains a consistent 185 mm whether you buy the entry-level EX or the top-tier SX(O) Connect.

If you prefer a premium hatchback experience but are worried about scrap damage, the [/cars/i20](/cars/i20) is another excellent option with 170 mm clearance, though the Exter remains the clear winner for purely rough-road drivability.
