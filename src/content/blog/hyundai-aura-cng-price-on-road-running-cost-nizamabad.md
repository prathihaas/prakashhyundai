### What Makes Up the On-Road Price?

The on-road price includes several components:

*   **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base price of the vehicle.
*   **Road Tax (RTO Registration):** In Telangana, this is a significant component, calculated as a percentage of the ex-showroom price. This can vary slightly based on the vehicle's price slab.
*   **Insurance:** Comprehensive motor insurance is mandatory. The cost depends on the variant, insurer, and add-ons you choose.
*   **Logistics/Handling Charges:** Minor charges for transporting the vehicle from the factory to the dealership.
*   **Fastag:** For electronic toll collection.

Combining these, the on-road price for a Hyundai Aura CNG in Nizamabad generally falls in the range of **Rs 9 Lakhs to Rs 10.7 Lakhs** as of August 2026.
