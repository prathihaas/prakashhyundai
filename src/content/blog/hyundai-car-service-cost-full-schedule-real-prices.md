## Hyundai Car Service Schedule: What to Expect

Hyundai follows a clear service schedule, typically every 10,000 km or 1 year, whichever comes first. The first few services are primarily inspections and fluid changes, while later services involve more component replacements.

### Initial Services (Free Labour)

These are crucial for checking initial wear and tear and ensuring everything is settling in correctly. You generally only pay for consumables like oil and filters.

*   **1st Service (1,500 km or 2 months):** Basic inspection, fluid level checks. (Labour free)
*   **2nd Service (10,000 km or 1 year):** Oil change, oil filter change, general check-up. (Labour free)
*   **3rd Service (20,000 km or 2 years):** Oil change, oil filter change, air filter check/replacement, general check-up. (Labour free)

### Periodic Paid Services

After the initial free labour services, each subsequent service will have a labour charge in addition to parts and consumables. These services are more comprehensive.

*   **4th Service (30,000 km or 3 years):** Engine oil and filter change, air filter replacement, fuel filter check, spark plug check, brake inspection, wheel alignment/balancing (if needed, extra cost).
*   **5th Service (40,000 km or 4 years):** This is often a more significant service. It includes everything from the 30k service, plus potential replacement of fuel filter, spark plugs, and a thorough inspection of suspension, belts, and hoses.
*   **Subsequent Services:** The pattern largely repeats, with major components like timing belts (if applicable) and coolant being replaced at specific higher mileage intervals (e.g., 80,000 km or 1,00,000 km).

## Factors Influencing Your Hyundai Car Service Cost

Several elements can impact the final bill for your Hyundai's service:

### 1. Car Model and Engine Type

An <a href="/cars/exter">Exter</a>, with its smaller engine, will naturally have lower service costs than an <a href="/cars/alcazar">Alcazar</a>, which uses more oil and larger filters. Diesel engines often have different (and sometimes more expensive) filters and require specific diesel engine oil, leading to marginally higher costs compared to petrol variants.

### 2. Service Interval and Mileage

As your car clocks more kilometres, certain components wear out and need replacement. The 40,000 km and 80,000 km services are generally more expensive as they involve more comprehensive checks and part replacements like fuel filters, spark plugs, and sometimes even drive belts.

### 3. Parts Replacement (Beyond Standard)

During routine checks, our technicians might identify worn-out components like brake pads, clutch plates, suspension bushes, or tyres. Replacing these will add to your bill. We always consult you before undertaking any additional repairs.

### 4. Additional Services

Things like wheel alignment and balancing, AC gas top-up, interior cleaning, or paint protection treatments are usually optional and come with extra charges. While not mandatory for basic service, they contribute to the overall well-being and appearance of your car.

### 5. Location
