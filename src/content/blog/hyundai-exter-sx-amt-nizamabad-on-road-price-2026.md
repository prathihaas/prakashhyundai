## Hyundai Exter SX AMT On-Road Price Nizamabad: What You Really Pay

When you're looking at buying a new car, the 'ex-showroom' price is just the beginning. For residents in Nizamabad and surrounding areas like Kamareddy or Banswada, the 'on-road price' is what truly matters. This includes several components beyond the basic vehicle cost:

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the manufacturer's price for the vehicle, before any taxes or registration.
2.  **Road Tax (RTO Charges):** In Telangana, this typically adds about 10-13% of the ex-showroom price. This varies slightly based on the vehicle's price slab.
3.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive motor insurance is mandatory and its cost depends on the insured declared value (IDV) of the car, your chosen insurer, and add-ons.
4.  **TCS (Tax Collected at Source):** A small percentage, usually 1%, on vehicles priced above ₹10 Lakhs.
5.  **Fastag:** Essential for toll plazas.
6.  **Registration Charges:** Fees for registering your vehicle with the local RTO.
