## Hyundai Exter AMT On-Road Price Nizamabad 2026: Variant-wise Breakdown

The on-road price is more than just the ex-showroom cost. It includes several mandatory components that vary by state and city. For Telangana, these typically include:

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the manufacturer's suggested retail price.
2.  **Road Tax (RTO):** Calculated as a percentage of the ex-showroom price, this goes to the Telangana Transport Department. For new cars in Telangana, it's typically around 10-13% for vehicles under Rs 10 Lakhs.
3.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is mandatory. Costs vary based on the variant, insurer, and add-ons (e.g., zero depreciation, engine protection).
4.  **Registration Charges:** Fees for vehicle registration.
5.  **FasTag:** Mandatory electronic toll collection tag.
6.  **Logistics/Handling Charges:** Dealer-specific charges.
7.  **Optional Accessories/Extended Warranty:** These can add to the final cost but offer value.

## Understanding the Hyundai Exter AMT: Features and Benefits

The Exter, even in its AMT avatar, packs a punch. It's designed to offer a robust feel with its SUV stance, 185mm ground clearance, and a host of features. The AMT (Automated Manual Transmission) system means you get the convenience of an automatic without the higher cost or complexity of a CVT or torque converter. It's essentially a manual gearbox with an intelligent system that handles clutch and gear shifts for you.
